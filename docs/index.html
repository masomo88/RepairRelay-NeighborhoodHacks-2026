THE OPPORTUNITY

Every neighbourhood has things worth fixing.

Repair events can be hard to coordinate: organisers need to know what is coming, which skills are available, and what needs a safety review. Repair Relay makes that work visible in one place.

Context: ISTAT reports that only 19.6% of Italian provincial-capital municipalities had repair or preparation-for-reuse centres in 2024. Read the source ↗
